# 2026-10-20: The search (Milestone 2, step 2)

## Goal
Turn the evaluation into a move choice: look ahead, assume both sides play
their best, and pick the move whose worst case is best. Also handle every
way a game ends, so the engine knows a draw when it sees one.

## What was done
- `src/search.rs`:
  - **Negamax with alpha-beta.** Each side picks the move that's worst
    for the other. Once a move is refuted, its remaining replies are skipped.
  - **Iterative deepening.** Depth 1, then 2, then 3. Last depth's best line
    is tried first, which makes alpha-beta cut much sooner.
  - **Quiescence.** At the end of a line, captures are played out until the
    position is quiet. The side to move may decline them ("stand pat");
    in check, every escape is tried.
  - **Move ordering.** Last depth's best move first, then captures (biggest
    victim, cheapest attacker: MVV-LVA) and promotions, then quiet moves.
  - **Results.** Checkmate scores `30000 - plies`, so quicker mates are
    preferred and being mated later is preferred to sooner. Stalemate,
    repetition, the 50-move rule and insufficient material score 0.
  - **No clock inside.** The search asks a `should_stop` question every
    2,048 positions and reports after each depth. Depth 1 always finishes,
    so there's always a move.
- `src/board.rs`: `repeats` (same position for the repetition rule) and
  `insufficient_material`.
- `src/uci.rs`: the game's positions are now kept (for repetitions).
  `go depth N` prints an `info` line per depth (score, nodes, speed, best
  line) and `bestmove`. `go` with no depth uses depth 5 until clocks
  arrive. `eval` prints the evaluation from White's side.

## Problems and fixes
- A first test, "Black is mated in one", wasn't. Black could push a pawn to
  give the king an escape square. Replaced with a position where the reply
  is forced (rook on the seventh, Kg8, Rb8 mate).
- The `eval` test expected "+9.xx" for an extra queen. The real value is
  +8.90 because the queen on d1 loses a little for being near the edge. The
  test now checks "more than 8 pawns".
- A first draft of the repetition test would have compared a position with
  itself. Rewrote it around a small helper that sets up the history.

## How it was verified
- 18 new tests (80 now pass): mate in 1 and 2 (the two-rook ladder, with the
  line played out to a real checkmate), being mated is negative, mate
  preferred to stalemate, takes a free queen, doesn't grab a defended pawn
  with the queen even at depth 1 (quiescence), saves an attacked queen,
  the 50-move rule turns a rook-up position into 0, insufficient material,
  knights out and back repeat the position, stop is honoured after depth 1,
  no move when checkmated, `go depth` and `eval` over UCI.
- Proved the tests can fail. Each rule was switched off in turn and at
  least one test failed each time: quiescence (the defended-pawn test),
  repetition, the 50-move rule, insufficient material, and mate distance
  (5 tests).
- In release, from the start position: depth 6 in 0.7 s (570,765
  positions, about 800,000 a second), best line 1. Nc3 Nc6 2. Nf3 Nf6
  3. d4 d5. Kiwipete reaches depth 6 in 6 s.
- `cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings`, WASM build.

## Impact
Stargazer chooses moves. A GUI can play it now, at a fixed depth. Step 3
adds clocks and `stop` so it plays properly under time controls.
