# 2026-11-08: Mobility

## Goal
Add the third evaluation term from the design (DESIGN.md section 4,
decided 2026-11-07): reward pieces that have squares to go to.

## What was done
- `mobility()` in `src/eval.rs` counts, for every knight, bishop, rook
  and queen, the **safe** squares it reaches: not held by its own
  pieces, and not attacked by an enemy pawn (stepping there would lose
  the piece to a pawn). Squares with enemy pieces on them count, since
  they can be captured.
- Each piece scores points per square above or below a usual count,
  so a typical piece is near 0 and material and the square tables keep
  their meaning:

  | Piece | Usual squares | Per square, middlegame / endgame |
  |---|---|---|
  | Knight | 4 | 4 / 4 |
  | Bishop | 7 | 5 / 5 |
  | Rook | 7 | 2 / 4 |
  | Queen | 14 | 1 / 2 |

  A knight in the corner (2 squares) scores -8; a bishop on a long open
  diagonal (11) +20. Rooks and queens count more in the endgame, when
  the board opens up.
- It uses the same attack functions as move generation (ray walking for
  bishops, rooks and queens), as decided: magic bitboards only if the
  slowdown costs strength.

## Problems and fixes
- My first test of the pawn rule put black pawns on c7 and f7, which
  guard b6, d6, e6 and g6; none of those is a square a knight on d4
  reaches, so the test asked for the wrong number. It now uses pawns on
  d7 and c4, which take away c6, e6 and b3.

## How it was verified
- New tests: `a_free_piece_scores_more_than_a_hemmed_in_one` (a bishop
  shut in behind its own pawns, a knight in the corner and the centre),
  `squares_an_enemy_pawn_guards_dont_count`,
  `rooks_and_queens_count_more_in_the_endgame` (which also checks that
  Black's pieces count the other way), and a middlegame position with
  pieces of both colours added to the mirrored-positions test. Each was
  seen failing when its rule was broken (a piece's points set to 0, the
  rook's endgame weight made equal to its middlegame one, enemy-pawn
  squares counted as safe, both colours scored as White), then passing.
- 125 tests pass; `cargo clippy`, `cargo fmt`, the wasm builds and the
  browser smoke test are clean.
- Bench changes, as expected for an evaluation change: 282819 nodes
  becomes **296359**. Speed, six runs each, alternating: median about
  394,000 positions a second against 415,000 for main, about 5% slower.
- SPRT [0, 10] at 8+0.08 against main (`sprt-42429383228-1`):
  **passed**, +74.0 ± 29.6 Elo over 389 games (178 wins, 113 draws,
  98 losses; LLR 2.98), in about 13 minutes. The second-largest gain of
  Milestone 4 after the transposition table, for about 5% of speed.

## Impact
The engine now prefers active pieces to passive ones, which the square
tables could only guess at from where a piece stands, not from what is
in its way: about +74 Elo. A 5% slower search was a good trade, so
magic bitboards stay parked (DECISIONS.md, 2026-11-07: only if the
slowdown costs strength).
